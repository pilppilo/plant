# Plant agent instructions

Read `PROTOCOL.md` completely before creating or changing a planning document.

- Treat the plan index as the routing source of truth.
- Do not implement from a document whose status is `draft` or `proposed`
  unless the operator explicitly directs an experiment.
- Do not promote a document to `accepted`, `ready`, `implemented`, or
  `verified` without the authority and evidence required by the protocol.
- Preserve accepted decisions. Change them by adding a superseding decision.
- Keep observations and workstream notes separate from requirements.
- Link claims of completion to concrete code, tests, or external evaluation.
- Prefer one active editor per document. Coordinate shared discoveries through
  workstream logs and narrowly scoped changes.
- Report conflicts with exact quotations and document IDs. Do not resolve
  product-direction conflicts by inference.
